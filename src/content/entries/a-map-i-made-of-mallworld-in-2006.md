A pen-drawn city plan on a worn sheet, dated 2006 in the post title — drawn
years before the phenomenon had a community. Labeled places include a "Mall
Building" with arcade, underground markets and underground transport, a
college, a church, a cafe, a "Bike Park," a "War Memorial-esque tower," a
"Sears tower like building," a river crossed by several bridges, a "Golden
City" overlook, and a southern border marked Mexico with a note pointing "to
South America." Marginal notes question the geography ("What's here?").
