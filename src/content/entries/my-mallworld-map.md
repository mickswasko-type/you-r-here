A green-marker map ringed by handwritten annotations, watermarked "Mallworld."
Labeled places include the Mall, an Airport, a School, a Hospital, a Hotel, a
Beach, an Amusement Park, a water park with safety nets, "Infinite Toilets"
("dirty, wet, weird"), a junkyard, a trailer park, and an antique shop where
"the Archiver" works. The margin notes read like field guidance: "I always
begin in the Auditorium," "If anything catastrophic happens, meet us at the
boxcar," "Everyone sticks gum to gum tree."
