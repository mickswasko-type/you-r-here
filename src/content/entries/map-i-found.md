Two pencil plan drawings on one sheet for an amusement park titled "Pleasure
Harbour," divided into "Section 1: Harbour" and "Section 2: Monoline." Labeled
areas include an Entrance, Gift Shops, Gentle Rides, a "Thrill Zone" with
coasters, water rides and pools, and a monoline track connecting the sections.
Unlike most maps in this archive, it depicts a single attraction in plan view
rather than a whole region.
