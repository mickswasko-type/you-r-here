A color map in marker and crayon covering a whole dream region, of which the
mall is one landmark among many. Labeled places include a City, Suburbs, a
School, a Beach, a Fairground, an Underground Parking Garage, a "Toilet Maze
Shopping Plaza," a "Party Mansion," a "Mystery Farmhouse," an "Impossible
Bridge," a "Stairway to Sky," and a "Tree Apartments Campground." A river and
a "Scenic Lake Route" divide the region, with a compass rose in the corner.
